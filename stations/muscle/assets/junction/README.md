# Neuromuscular junction cutaway

`neuromuscular_junction.glb` is generated by `generate_junction.py` using Python's standard library only. The geometry is original procedural work; no external mesh, texture, or image generator is used. Run this from the repository root to reproduce it:

```sh
python3 stations/muscle/assets/junction/generate_junction.py
```

## Scale and layout

Coordinates use **1 GLB unit = 1 µm**, with `+Y` up and `+Z` toward the camera in the front review view. The model spans approximately 9.6 × 10.2 × 6.4 µm. It is an educational cutaway; the synaptic cleft and receptor assemblies are enlarged for readability and the model should not be treated as a histology or molecular scale reference.

- `Terminal` is the volumetric cutaway motor bouton, centered at `(0, 1.95, 0) µm`; its rounded shell opens toward the review camera. `Axon` is a separate narrow shaft extending upward from the bouton; `TerminalInterior` is recessed under the open face.
- `Vesicle_01`–`Vesicle_07` are independent children of `Terminal`. Their local translations are rest positions. Move each vesicle in local `−Y` toward the cleft for release; the node owns its vesicle membrane, and `ACh_*` children move with it.
- `SynapticCleft` is a separate, translucent volume centered at `(0, .54, 0) µm`; it fills the curved interval between bouton and postsynaptic surface. Its visible thickness is intentionally exaggerated so the release route can be read.
- `MuscleFiber` is a thick curved cutaway segment. `Sarcolemma` is a separate rounded cap with visible wall thickness, fitted over the fiber's upper contour. `Receptor_01`–`Receptor_06` are separate channel anchors across that surface; each has five `Subunit_*` meshes and a central `Pore` mesh.
- `TTubule` is a hollow, open-ended invagination from the sarcolemma into the fiber. Its mouth pivot is at the membrane; its lumen extends down to about `Y = −2.72 µm`.
- `SR` groups two terminal cisternae (`SR_Cisterna_L`, `SR_Cisterna_R`) beside the T-tubule. `Ca2_ParticleSocket_*` nodes are named empty transforms carrying `particle: Ca2+` metadata for release animation. Their coordinates are the particle spawn positions. Small `Ca2_Particle_*` meshes show the stored ions at rest.

The handedness is a schematic triad: nerve terminal above the postsynaptic sarcolemma, with the T-tubule entering the muscle cutaway and sarcoplasmic-reticulum cisternae flanking it. Warm copper/rose identifies nerve and muscle membranes; cyan/teal identifies receptor and excitation structures; pale calcium spheres are distinct from both.
