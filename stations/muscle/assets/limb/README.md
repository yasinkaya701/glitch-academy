# Arm and elbow · Section E

`arm_biceps.glb` is original, deterministic procedural anatomy generated by `generate_arm.py` with Python's standard library. It uses the issue's required metric scale and contains no external image, mesh, text, or AI-generated content.

## Rig and movement
- `Humerus` runs from the shoulder at approximately Y=0.335 m to the elbow at the origin.
- `Elbow` is the forearm pivot at `(0, 0, 0)`, with hinge axis Z. `Radius`, `Ulna`, wrist ends, and forearm tendons are children, so one rotation carries the forearm.
- `Biceps` and `Triceps` are separate named mesh nodes. Biceps shortening flexes the elbow; triceps shortening extends it. Each can be independently scaled along local Y. `Tendon_Biceps` and `Tendon_Triceps` connect their muscle bellies to the radius/olecranon.
- Positive `Elbow.rotation.z` lifts the forearm in the anterior view; the model's neutral pose is slightly extended.

Run `python3 stations/muscle/assets/limb/generate_arm.py` to rebuild the GLB.

`preview_arm.gd` renders two temporary Godot compositions: biceps flexion and triceps extension. The preview PNGs are engine renders of this asset, not running-mission screenshots.

## Review captures
- `previews/biceps_flexion.png`: anterior view, forearm flexed around the elbow pivot.
- `previews/triceps_extension.png`: posterior view, forearm extended around the same pivot.

Rebuild with Godot 4.7.2: `godot --headless --editor --path . --import --quit`, then run the preview script with the desktop renderer enabled. The generator and this model are original work; there are no third-party mesh or texture dependencies.
