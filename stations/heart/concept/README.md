# Heart Room concept studies

These files preserve the visual iterations as the brief changed. They are exploratory only; none is approved as a final concept or production anatomy.

- `heart_concept_holographic.png` and `heart_concept_micro_world.png` are from the superseded brief.
- `heart_concept_01_perfusion_lab.png`, `heart_concept_02_open_bench.png`, and `heart_concept_03_precision_rig.png` are rough perfusion-bench studies for the updated brief.
- `archive/heart_base_raw.png` preserves the earlier generated heart image from issue #3's superseded request.

Visual QA found unclear cannula routing, similar compositions in concepts 01 and 02, and a nonblank monitor in concept 03. Use these only to discuss mood and framing. Do not use them as clinical references or ship them as approved art. The referenced heart GLB and review PNGs were absent from the current checkout when these notes were written.
