# Seasons observatory Earth asset

`earth_albedo_4k.jpg` is the 4096 × 2048 equirectangular surface texture used by both globe meshes in the seasons observatory. `earth_source.png` preserves the original 1774 × 887 generated artwork.

`sun_photosphere_4k.jpg` is the 4096 × 2048 emissive surface used by the Sun sphere. `sun_source.png` preserves the original generated artwork.

The Earth and Sun source artwork was generated for this project with OpenAI ImageGen. The 4K files are Lanczos-resampled from their source images, so the larger dimensions do not imply additional source detail. The Earth map is a visually rich educational visualization rather than a navigational or scientific basemap.

`axial_tilt_key_art.png` is the 3D key art for the seasons mission. It illustrates the parallel 23.5° axial tilt at the June and December orbital positions; the interactive lesson uses the real-time scene in `../observatory_diagram.gd`.
